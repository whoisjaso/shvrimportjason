# Privacy policy: HCP Job Costing Export for SHVR

Last updated: November 12, 2026

HCP Job Costing Export is a Chrome extension that exports your own Housecall Pro jobs and job costs to spreadsheet files on your computer.

## What the extension reads

- **Your Housecall Pro API key**, which you paste in. It is stored only in your browser's local extension storage so you don't have to paste it again. Removing the extension deletes it.
- **Your jobs**, from Housecall Pro's public API (`api.housecallpro.com`), using that API key. Jobs include customer names, contact details, addresses, dates and amounts.
- **Each job's materials and miscellaneous costs**, from Housecall Pro (`pro.housecallpro.com`), using the sign-in you already have in your browser. The extension never reads, copies or stores your Housecall Pro password or sign-in cookie; your browser attaches it to those requests the same way it does when you use Housecall Pro.

## What it does with that data

It turns the data into three CSV files, zips them, and saves the zip to your Downloads folder. While an export runs, progress is kept in your browser's local extension storage so an interrupted export can resume; starting a new export replaces it, and removing the extension deletes it.

## What it never does

- It sends no data anywhere except Housecall Pro's own servers, and only to read your data.
- It has no server of its own, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- It does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
- It does not change anything in your Housecall Pro account. Every request it makes is a read.
